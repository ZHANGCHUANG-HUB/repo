# 产品理解与锁定

检查类别、轮廓、尺寸比例、包装结构、材质、主辅色、Logo、标签、文字区域、开合方式、表面反射和易变形部位。输出产品事实、视觉识别、可用卖点与证据、受众与场景假设、Product Lock、待确认信息与风险。

## Product Lock 模板

```text
Use the uploaded product reference as the single source of truth. Preserve the exact product silhouette, proportions, package construction, material, primary and secondary colors, logo placement, label layout, typography zones, closure design, seams, edges, and all visible identifying details across every shot. Keep the same product variant and scale relationship. Do not redesign, simplify, invent, translate, replace, mirror, or distort any logo, label, package text, cap, seal, pattern, or structural feature. If small text cannot be reproduced reliably, keep the original reference area visually intact and do not invent substitute words.
```

按产品补充 3–8 个不可变特征，不要添加参考图中不存在的细节。不得编造医疗或性能功效、成分比例、产地、年份、工艺、认证、奖项、销量、排名、评价、折扣或价格。无法确认时改写为感官、场景或设计表达。
